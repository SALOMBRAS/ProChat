## OpenWA identity patterns

ChatPro reimplements, without copying source code, the WhatsApp identifier
classification and LID-to-phone resolution patterns documented from OpenWA,
commit `c7e0ff3248ef7176d006e2ff59ad9710ff0759f1`. Reference files:
`src/engine/identity/wa-id.ts`, `src/engine/identity/lid-mapping-store.service.ts`,
`src/engine/adapters/baileys-session-store.ts`, and
`src/engine/adapters/whatsapp-web-js.adapter.ts`.
